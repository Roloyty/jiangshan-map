苏曼殊中日浮沉与南游行迹（1884年 — 1918年 · 明治十七年至民国七年）

Su Manshu: Between Japan and China (1884 CE – 1918 CE · Meiji Japan to Republic of China)

苏曼殊中日浮沉与南游行迹 · 逐站时空坐标、古今地名与史料诗词详解（9 站）

第 1 站 横滨（今日本横滨）· 生于横滨 Yokohama (Yokohama, Japan) — Born in Yokohama
1884年（明治十七年） · 在地图定位此站 →

生于日本横滨，父苏杰生为广东香山茶商，母为日本人；身世难言，一生以"难言之恫"自伤。

Born in Yokohama to a Cantonese tea merchant and a Japanese mother, he carried an unspoken family wound all his life.

第 2 站 东京早稻田（今日本东京）· 东京求学 Waseda, Tokyo (Tokyo, Japan) — Student in Tokyo
1902年（明治三十五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

就读横滨大同学校后赴东京，入早稻田大学预科，与冯自由、陈独秀辈游，投身青年会。

After the Datong School in Yokohama he entered Waseda’s preparatory course in Tokyo, falling in with the young revolutionaries around Chen Duxiu.

第 3 站 上海（今上海）· 国民日日报 Shanghai (Shanghai, China) — The National Daily
1903年（光绪二十九年） · 在地图定位此站 →

回国任《国民日日报》翻译，与陈独秀合译雨果《悲惨世界》；旋赴惠州断发为僧，未几还俗，行迹如谜。

Back in China he translated for the National Daily and worked with Chen Duxiu on a Chinese version of Les Misérables; a brief monkhood at Huizhou followed, and the rest of his life kept the enigma.

第 4 站 香港（今香港）· 南游学梵 Hong Kong (Hong Kong, China) — South for Sanskrit
1904年（光绪三十年） · 在地图定位此站 →

自香港浮海南游，历暹罗、锡兰，从锡兰长老习梵文；此后译《梵文典》，融印度之学于诗情。

Sailing south from Hong Kong through Siam to Ceylon, he studied Sanskrit with an elder monk — the seed of his Sanskrit primer and his India-tinged verse.

第 5 站 东京（今日本东京）· 与太炎同寓 Tokyo (Tokyo, Japan) — Lodging with Zhang Taiyan
1907年（明治四十年） · 在地图定位此站 →

再赴东京，与章太炎同寓，共拟《儆告十方佛弟子书》；《文学因缘》《梵文典》次第成书。

In Tokyo he shared lodgings with Zhang Taiyan, co-authoring open letters to Buddhists, and brought out his Literary Bonds and Sanskrit studies.

第 6 站 上海太平洋报（今上海）· 断鸿零雁记 Pacific News, Shanghai (Shanghai, China) — The Lone Goose
1912年（民国元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

主《太平洋报》笔政，连载自传体小说《断鸿零雁记》，"乱世逃禅"之音倾动一时；南社诗人推为座上。

Editing the Pacific News, he serialized his autobiographical novel The Lone Goose, the voice of a monk adrift in revolution, and became the darling of the Southern Society.

第 7 站 东京（今日本东京）· 东渡养病 Tokyo (Tokyo, Japan) — Illness in Tokyo
1914年（大正三年） · 在地图定位此站 →

病体东渡养疴，刊《汉英三昧集》；诗画益苍凉，"春雨楼头尺八箫"之句传诵至今。

He convalesced in Tokyo and compiled a Chinese-English anthology; his poems of rain, shakuhachi and exile were on every young lip.

苏曼殊《本事诗》（1909）：“春雨楼头尺八箫，何时归看浙江潮？芒鞋破钵无人识，踏过樱花第几桥。”Su Manshu, "On Things as They Were" (1909): "Rain at the tower-head, a shakuhachi on the wind — when shall I go home to watch the Zhejiang tide? Straw sandals, a broken bowl, no one knows me; how many cherry bridges have I crossed?"
第 8 站 西湖（今浙江杭州）· 湖上萍踪 West Lake, Hangzhou (Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China) — Drifting by West Lake
1916年（民国五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

数游西湖，寓孤山，纵情诗画而贫病日深；与陈独秀、柳亚子书问不绝，自谓"遭逢世网，如落叶随风"。

He drifted to West Lake and Solitary Hill, poor and ailing, still exchanging letters with Chen Duxiu and Liu Yazi — a leaf on the wind, as he wrote.

第 9 站 上海广慈医院（今上海）· 病逝沪上 Sainte-Marie Hospital, Shanghai (Shanghai, China) — Death in Shanghai
1918年（民国七年） · 在地图定位此站 →

5月2日病逝于上海广慈医院，年三十五；葬西湖孤山，一生诗、画、译、禅俱绝，而情僧之名长留。

He died at Shanghai’s Sainte-Marie Hospital on 2 May 1918, aged thirty-five, and was buried at Solitary Hill on West Lake — poet, painter, translator, monk.
