Lu Xun: From Sendai to the New Culture Movement (1881 CE – 1936 CE · Late Qing to Republic of China)

鲁迅留学东瀛与呐喊行迹 · 逐站时空坐标、古今地名与史料诗词详解（11 站）

第 1 站 绍兴府（今浙江绍兴）· 生于绍兴·新台门 Shaoxing Prefecture (Shaoxing, Zhejiang) — Born in the Xintaimen Residence, Shaoxing
1881年（光绪七年） · 在地图定位此站 →

生于绍兴府会稽县东昌坊口新台门周家，幼从寿镜吾读于三味书屋；祖父科场案下狱、父亲病故，家道中落，常出入当铺与药铺之间。

Born into the Zhou family of the Xintaimen residence in Shaoxing, Lu Xun studied at the Sanwei Bookroom under Shou Jingwu; the fall of his family after his grandfather's imprisonment and his father's illness marked his childhood.

第 2 站 江宁（今江苏南京）· 南京求学·矿路学堂 Jiangning (Nanjing) (Nanjing, Jiangsu) — Studying Mining and Railways in Nanjing
1898年（光绪二十四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

考入江南水师学堂，旋改入江南陆师学堂附设矿务铁路学堂，读严译《天演论》，始接触进化论与新学。

Lu Xun entered the Jiangnan Naval Academy, then transferred to the School of Mines and Railways, where reading Yan Fu's translation of Evolution and Ethics opened him to new thought.

第 3 站 东京弘文学院（今日本东京）· 弘文学院·剪辫明志 Kobun Institute, Tokyo (Tokyo, Japan) — The Kobun Institute and the Cut Queue
1902年（明治三十五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

由江南督练公所派赴日本留学，入弘文学院普通科江南班；次年剪去发辫，摄“断发照”以明志。

Sent to Japan as a government student, Lu Xun joined the Kobun Institute; the next year he cut off his queue and had a photograph taken to mark his break with the old order.

鲁迅《自题小照》（1903）：“寄意寒星荃不察，我以我血荐轩辕。”Lu Xun, Inscription on a Photo (1903): "I offer my heart to the stars of home, though my countrymen cannot see; with my blood I pledge myself to the Yellow Emperor."
第 4 站 仙台医学专门学校（今日本仙台）· 仙台学医·藤野先生 Sendai Medical College (Sendai, Japan) — Medicine in Sendai and Professor Fujino
1904年（明治三十七年） · 在地图定位此站 →

转入仙台医学专门学校，从藤野严九郎改订讲义；日俄战争幻灯片中围观同胞被处决的麻木看客，令其深受刺激。

Lu Xun transferred to Sendai Medical College, where Fujino Genkurō corrected his lecture notes; a slide show of numb Chinese onlookers during the Russo-Japanese War shook him deeply.

鲁迅《藤野先生》：“他的性格，在我的眼里和心里是伟大的，虽然他的姓名并不为许多人所知道。”Lu Xun, Mr. Fujino: "His character was great in my eyes and in my heart, though his name is known to few."
第 5 站 东京（今日本东京）· 弃医从文·域外小说集 Tokyo (Tokyo, Japan) — Abandoning Medicine for Literature
1906年（明治三十九年） · 在地图定位此站 →

退学回东京，谋办杂志《新生》未成，与周作人合译《域外小说集》；1908 年起从章太炎先生听讲《说文解字》。

Leaving medicine, Lu Xun returned to Tokyo, planned a magazine that never launched, and co-translated Stories from Abroad with his brother; from 1908 he studied under the philologist Zhang Taiyan.

鲁迅《呐喊·自序》：“所以我们的第一要著，是在改变他们的精神，而善于改变精神的是，我那时以为当然要推文艺。”Lu Xun, Preface to Call to Arms: "The first task was to change their spirit, and literature, I then thought, was the best means for changing it."
第 6 站 杭州（今浙江杭州）· 归国执教·两级师范 Hangzhou (Hangzhou, Zhejiang) — Teaching in Hangzhou
1909年（宣统元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

归国任杭州浙江两级师范学堂化学、生理学教员，译《月界旅行》《地底旅行》诸书。

Back in China, Lu Xun taught chemistry and physiology at the Zhejiang Two-Level Normal School in Hangzhou, translating Jules Verne on the side.

第 7 站 绍兴府中学堂（今浙江绍兴）· 回绍兴·府中学堂 Shaoxing Prefectural Middle School (Shaoxing, Zhejiang) — Back to Shaoxing
1910年（宣统二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

任绍兴府中学堂监学；辛亥革命杭州光复时，组织学生武装演说队上街迎接。

Lu Xun served as proctor of the Shaoxing Prefectural Middle School; when the revolution reached Hangzhou he led his students into the streets.

第 8 站 北京（今北京）· 教育部佥事·狂人日记 Beijing (Beijing) — The Ministry of Education and A Madman's Diary
1912年（民国元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

随临时政府北迁，任教育部社会教育司佥事；1918 年在《新青年》发表中国第一篇现代白话小说《狂人日记》，始署名“鲁迅”，后有《阿Q正传》。

Moving to Beijing with the Republican government, Lu Xun served in the Ministry of Education; in 1918 he published China's first modern vernacular story, A Madman's Diary, under the pen name Lu Xun.

鲁迅《狂人日记》：“从来如此，便对么？”Lu Xun, A Madman's Diary: "It has always been so, therefore it is right?"
第 9 站 厦门大学（今福建厦门）· 厦门大学 Xiamen University (Xiamen, Fujian) — Professor at Xiamen
1926年（民国十五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

三一八惨案后南下，任厦门大学国文系教授，编讲义《中国文学史略》，写《从百草园到三味书屋》《藤野先生》诸篇。

After the March 18 Massacre, Lu Xun taught Chinese literature at Xiamen University, drafting a literary history and writing the essays of Dawn Blossoms Plucked at Dusk.

第 10 站 广州（今广东广州）· 广州中山大学 Canton (Guangzhou) (Guangzhou, Guangdong) — Sun Yat-sen University in Canton
1927年（民国十六年） · 在地图定位此站 →

任中山大学教务主任兼文学系主任；四一五清党后愤而辞职，蛰居白云楼整理《野草》《朝花夕拾》，九月离穗，十月抵上海。

Lu Xun served as dean of studies at Sun Yat-sen University; after the April 1927 purge he resigned in protest and left for Shanghai that autumn.

第 11 站 上海（今上海）· 上海十年·左联与逝世 Shanghai (Shanghai) — Ten Years in Shanghai
1927年（民国十六年） · 在地图定位此站 →

与许广平定居上海，常往内山书店；1930 年参与发起中国自由运动大同盟与左翼作家联盟；1936 年 10 月 19 日病逝于大陆新村寓所。

Settling in Shanghai, Lu Xun frequented the Uchiyama Bookstore; in 1930 he helped found the League of Left-Wing Writers. He died at home in Shanghai on October 19, 1936.

鲁迅《这也是生活》（1936）：“无穷的远方，无数的人们，都和我有关。”Lu Xun, This Is Life Too (1936): "The endless distance, the countless people, are all connected with me."
