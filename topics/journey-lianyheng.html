Lian Heng: A Taiwanese Historian Between Two Shores (1878 CE – 1936 CE · Japanese-era Taiwan to the Republic of China)

连横内渡与《台湾通史》行迹 · 逐站时空坐标、古今地名与史料诗词详解（12 站）

第 1 站 台南府（今台湾台南）· 生于台南马兵营 Tainan Prefecture (Tainan, Taiwan) — Born in the Mabingying Quarter, Tainan
1878年（光绪四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

生于台湾府台南马兵营，字武公，号雅堂；十三岁读父亲所购《台湾府志》，立志著台湾之史。

Lian Heng was born in Tainan to an old settler family; at thirteen, reading the prefectural gazetteer his father bought him, he resolved to write a history of Taiwan.

连横父训（见《连雅堂家传》）：“汝为台湾人，不可不知台湾事。”His father's charge, as recorded in the family memoir: "You are Taiwanese — you must know the affairs of Taiwan."
第 2 站 厦门（今福建厦门）· 乙未内渡 Amoy (Xiamen) (Xiamen, Fujian) — Crossing Over After the Cession
1895年（光绪二十一年） · 在地图定位此站 →

《马关条约》割台，全台乱离，随兄内渡福建避乱；翌年返台；1897 年一度赴上海、南京拟求学，旋奉母命回台成婚，任《台澎日报》汉文主笔。

When the Treaty of Shimonoseki ceded Taiwan, Lian crossed to Fujian with his elder brother amid the chaos, returning the next year; a brief study trip to Shanghai in 1897 ended with his recall home to marry and write for a Tainan newspaper.

第 3 站 厦门（今福建厦门）· 创办《福建日日新报》 Amoy (Xiamen) (Xiamen, Fujian) — The Fukien Jihjih Shinpao
1905年（光绪三十一年） · 在地图定位此站 →

1902 年赴福州、厦门应补行乡试不售；是年携眷再赴厦门，创办《福建日日新报》鼓吹排满，旋遭封禁，回台再主《台南新报》汉文部。

After failing the special provincial examination of 1902, Lian returned to Amoy with his family and ran an anti-Manchu paper until the authorities shut it down, going back to Tainan journalism.

第 4 站 台中（今台湾台中）· 移居台中·始著通史 Taichū (Taichung) (Taichung, Taiwan) — Beginning the General History of Taiwan
1908年（光绪三十四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

移居台中入《台湾新闻》汉文部，与林痴仙等诗人结栎社；在此发凡起例，始撰《台湾通史》；同年秋首度游日本。

Moving to Taichung to write for a newspaper, Lian joined the Li She poets' circle and there began drafting his General History of Taiwan, first visiting Japan that autumn.

第 5 站 上海（今上海）· 华侨联合会·主编《华侨杂志》 Shanghai (Shanghai) — The Overseas Chinese Federation in Shanghai
1912年（民国元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

3 月经神户赴上海，途经时当选福建省议会议员而辞就；入华侨联合会主编《华侨杂志》，周游杭州、南京、苏州诸地，谒秋瑾墓，与章太炎、张继订交。

Sailing via Kobe after the revolution, Lian was elected — and declined — a Fujian provincial assembly seat in transit; in Shanghai he edited the Overseas Chinese Federation's magazine, touring Hangzhou and Nanjing and befriending Zhang Taiyan.

第 6 站 北京（今北京）· 华侨选举·游历华北 Beijing (Beijing) — The Overseas Election in Beijing
1913年（民国二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

春赴北京参加华侨选举国会议员，游张家口与平汉铁路沿线、汉口、九江、芜湖、安庆；此行见闻后成《大陆游记》。

Lian went to Beijing in spring 1913 for the overseas Chinese congressional election, traveling on through Zhangjiakou, Hankou and the Yangtze ports; his notes became a celebrated travelogue.

第 7 站 吉林（今吉林吉林市）· 《新吉林报》主笔 Jilin (Jilin City, Jilin) — Editor in Jilin
1913年（民国二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

秋经奉天转赴吉林，任《新吉林报》主笔；次年春回北京入清史馆，尽阅台湾建省档案，冬倦游归台。

That autumn Lian went on to Jilin in Manchuria as a newspaper editor, then returned briefly to the Qing History Institute in Beijing before sailing home at the end of 1914.

第 8 站 台北（今台湾台北）· 《台湾通史》成书 Taihoku (Taipei) (Taipei, Taiwan) — Completing the General History of Taiwan
1918年（民国七年） · 在地图定位此站 →

返台续撰，1918 年成《台湾通史》三十六卷，1920 至 1921 年分三册刊行；章太炎许为民族精神之所附，必传之作。

Home again, Lian finished his thirty-six-chapter General History of Taiwan in 1918, published in three volumes from 1920; Zhang Taiyan hailed it as a vessel of national spirit.

第 9 站 东京（今日本东京）· 携眷游日本 Tokyo (Tokyo, Japan) — Travels in Japan
1923年（大正十二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

与夫人沈少云东游日本；长子连震东时留学东京庆应义塾大学，随侍父母漫游镰仓、箱根。

Lian toured Japan with his wife, joined by their son Lian Zhendong, then a student at Keio University, on excursions to Kamakura and Hakone.

第 10 站 杭州西湖（今浙江杭州）· 移居西湖 West Lake, Hangzhou (Hangzhou, Zhejiang) — A House by West Lake
1926年（民国十五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

夏携眷移居杭州西湖，编《宁南诗草》，以了“他日移家湖上住”之愿；翌年春因北伐战事返台。

In 1926 Lian moved his household to West Lake in Hangzhou, fulfilling an old poetic vow, until the Northern Expedition's turmoil sent the family back to Taiwan the next spring.

连横《西湖游罢》（1912）：“一春旧梦散如烟，三月桃花扑酒船。他日移家湖上住，青山青史各千年。”Lian Heng, after boating on West Lake: "One spring's old dream scatters like mist, peach blossoms drift against the wine boat; someday I shall move my home onto the lake — green hills and green history, a thousand years each."
第 11 站 上海（今上海）· 定居上海 Shanghai (Shanghai) — Settling in Shanghai for Good
1933年（民国二十二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

7 月携妻女移居上海，自言“余此次归国，欲为永居之计”；先是 1931 年已命震东回国投效，谒张继于南京。

In July 1933 Lian moved to Shanghai with wife and daughter to live out his days in what he called his homeland; two years earlier he had sent his son to serve the Republic.

第 12 站 上海（今上海）· 病逝沪上·遗命光复 Shanghai (Shanghai) — Dying in Shanghai, Foreseeing Recovery
1936年（民国二十五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

1935 年偕夫人游关中；1936 年 6 月 28 日因肝癌病逝上海寓所，年五十九。

After a final journey to the old capitals of Guanzhong in 1935, Lian died of liver cancer in Shanghai on June 28, 1936, aged fifty-eight.

连横弥留嘱子连震东（见《连雅堂家传》）：“今寇焰迫人，中日终必一战，光复台湾即其时也，汝其勉之！”His dying words to his son, as recorded in the family memoir: "The enemy's fury presses on; Japan and China must fight at last, and Taiwan's recovery will come with it — do your part!"
