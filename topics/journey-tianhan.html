Tian Han: Tokyo, the Southern Society and the Anthem (1898 CE – 1968 CE · Late Qing to People’s Republic)

田汉东京学剧与南国行迹 · 逐站时空坐标、古今地名与史料诗词详解（10 站）

第 1 站 长沙县田家塅（今湖南长沙）· 生于田家塅 Tianjiaduan, Changsha (Changsha, Hunan, China) — Born on a Hunan Farm
1898年（光绪二十四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

生于长沙县东乡贫农之家，九岁丧父；母夜说戏文为启蒙，少年与友易号"英雄怀汉"，自此名田汉。

Born to a poor farming family in Changsha County and orphaned at nine, he took the name Han ("Han" for the Han people) with three schoolmates in a pact of national resolve.

第 2 站 长沙师范学校（今湖南长沙）· 师范发轫 Changsha Normal School (Changsha, Hunan, China) — The Normal School Years
1912年（民国元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

入长沙师范学校，校长徐特立；京剧处女作《新教子》刊于《长沙日报》，继作《新桃花扇》讥袁世凯。

At the Changsha normal school under Xu Teli his first playlet appeared in the press; a second dramatized resistance to Yuan Shikai.

第 3 站 东京高等师范（今日本东京）· 负笈东瀛 Tokyo Higher Normal School (Tokyo, Japan) — Off to Tokyo
1916年（大正五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

随舅父易象赴日，入东京高等师范；与郭沫若、张资平辈订交，醉心易卜生与惠特曼。

He sailed for Japan with his uncle and entered the Tokyo Higher Normal School, befriending Guo Moruo and falling for Ibsen and Whitman.

第 4 站 东京（今日本东京）· 三叶集与创造社 Tokyo (Tokyo, Japan) — Three Leaves and the Creation Society
1920年（大正九年） · 在地图定位此站 →

与郭沫若、宗白华通信集《三叶集》出版，为五四新诗论之名篇；参与发起创造社，剧作《咖啡店之一夜》起步。

His correspondence with Guo Moruo and Zong Baihua appeared as Three Leaves, a manifesto of May Fourth poetry, as the Creation Society was founded.

第 5 站 上海中华书局（今上海）· 归国办南国 Zhonghua Book Company, Shanghai (Shanghai, China) — Home to Found Nanguo
1922年（民国十一年） · 在地图定位此站 →

携妻易漱瑜回国，任中华书局编辑；1924年自费创《南国》半月刊，"欲在沉闷的中国新文坛鼓动一种清新芳烈的艺术空气"。

Returning with his wife Yi Shuyu, he edited for Zhonghua Book Company and in 1924 founded the self-financed Nanguo magazine to stir "a fresh and fiery air" into Chinese letters.

第 6 站 上海（今上海）· 南国社 Shanghai (Shanghai, China) — The Southern Society
1928年（民国十七年） · 在地图定位此站 →

与徐悲鸿、欧阳予倩创南国艺术学院，继立南国社；率团巡演沪宁穗，"不问学历，但取天才"，穷干苦干为在野戏剧精神。

With Xu Beihong and Ouyang Yuqian he founded the Southern Society, touring Shanghai, Nanjing and Guangzhou on shoestrings with an eye for genius over credentials.

第 7 站 上海（今上海）· 义勇军进行曲 Shanghai (Shanghai, China) — The March of the Volunteers
1935年（民国二十四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

年初为电影《风云儿女》主题歌作词——《义勇军进行曲》，聂耳谱曲；二月被捕，囚南京，出狱后转赴各地从事救亡戏剧。

His lyrics for the film Children of Troubled Times became the March of the Volunteers (music by Nie Er) — in February he was arrested and jailed at Nanjing, and on release turned to resistance theatre.

田汉《义勇军进行曲》（1935）：“起来！不愿做奴隶的人们！把我们的血肉，筑成我们新的长城！”Tian Han, March of the Volunteers (1935): "Arise! All who refuse to be slaves! With our flesh and blood, build our new Great Wall!"
第 8 站 武汉三厅（今湖北武汉）· 三厅抗敌 Wuhan (Wuhan, Hubei, China) — Wartime Wuhan
1938年（民国二十七年） · 在地图定位此站 →

任军委会政治部三厅六处处长，与郭沫若共掌抗敌演剧、歌咏之业，组织十队演剧队奔赴各战区。

As a department head under Guo Moruo in the political third department, he sent drama and song troupes out to every war zone.

第 9 站 北平（今北京）· 国歌定响 Beiping (Beijing, China) — The Anthem Set
1949年（1949年） · 在地图定位此站 →

出席新政协，《义勇军进行曲》定为代国歌；此后主戏剧改革，任中国戏剧家协会主席，作《关汉卿》《谢瑶环》。

At the new People’s Political Consultative Conference his March became the national anthem; he went on to lead drama reform and write Guan Hanqing and Xie Yaohuan.

第 10 站 北京狱中（今北京）· 含冤病逝 A Beijing Hospital (Beijing, China) — Death in Custody
1968年（1968年） · 在地图定位此站 →

文革中因《谢瑶环》被诬为"大毒草"，1966年遭秘密监禁，1968年12月10日含冤死于狱中，化名"李伍"；1979年平反昭雪。

Denounced over Xie Yaohuan in the Cultural Revolution, he was secretly imprisoned in 1966 and died in custody on 10 December 1968 under a false name; he was rehabilitated in 1979.
